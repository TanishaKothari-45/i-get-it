# Assessment A, second run (9 Oct, 00:10): the link-to-wall path at 390 px

Read-only. Walked the live site with the helper (A2main, A2x, A2img, A2pics in the scratchpad) on a fresh headless phone profile. Did not sign up, pay, or type into Ask. Formed every score before opening the 23:20 critique.

## 1. Design specificity verdict

**Specific, with one seam that isn't visual.** The chapter frames are the most specific thing in the product: one flat ink colour, paper grain, a marker-stroke key phrase ("Your money goes to whoever owned the slice you bought." on indigo), a tilted printed label, a plate with a mat and offset shadow. The Shelf is a real object (cloth, spine, paper label, plank), not a grid of cards. Done and Sign-in now wear the same hand: pressed marigold, 2.5 px ink, Newsreader for the reassurance. The landing was always this.

The seam: the print-shop promise is "already there, like a Reel." Measured tonight, every picture on this path is a 1792×1344 PNG of 2.4 to 3.9 MB, shown at 343 CSS px. The carousel's first three cards are 11 MB; chapter 1 is 19.6 MB across six pictures; the Done wall card carries one more. On the fast line the helper runs on, the carousel plates were still blank at 4 s (A2img/i1) and the Done wall's picture never appeared in 2.5 s (A2main/15). On Jordan's 4G at 11pm, the specificity the design earned is spent waiting for grey rectangles. The design is not generic; its delivery is.

## 3. Cognitive load per screen

- **Done (Operate).** Make a free account, Back to the handbook, See pricing, Too easy / Just right / Lost me, Teach it back, The Shelf, ← Handbook, ☰ (opens 7 more links), wordmark = 11 visible + 7. Decision point over 4. The rating chips (Done.tsx:109–120) and Teach it back (Done.tsx:121) sit on a screen whose job is one yes/no.
- **Sign-in (Operate).** Email me a code, Not now, I already have a password, ← Back, The Shelf, ☰ = 6. The ☰ and The Shelf are noise at the email moment.
- **Plan after Not now (Operate).** Up next button, action-bar button (same action), chapter 1 "Read it again ›", "Jump to next" (3 topics), 3 quiet links, ☰ = 10. The two main-looking buttons are the declined action.
- **Shelf (Operate).** Surprise me + 6 anchor chips before any book, then 31 books in 6 rows. Decision point over 4 at the chip row; acceptable for a browse screen. Book titles at 12.5 px and goals at 9.9 px ("for: Cook it confi…" truncated, A2main/24) add read load.

## 4. Emotional journey

- **Peak:** frame 6, cream, `size-xl`: "So the company gets ₹150 crore, which is 30%. The family and early investors take home ₹350 crore, which is 70%." (A2main/11). That is the "I get it" the product is named for, and the Done outcome line lands it again: "You can now look at any IPO and say how much of the money raised goes to the company and how much goes to the people selling." Outcome-first on Done was the right call.
- **Valley:** two, both network-shaped. The landing's "It opens instantly" sits above three blank plates (A2main/01, 02; still one blank at 4 s in A2img/i1). On Done, the first thing inside the printed wall card is a grey box (A2main/15) and the reassuring sentence is under the button.
- **End (peak-end rule):** for the signer-up, the end is the email screen, which is calm and well-anchored. For the 100% so far who do not sign up, the end is the plan with "Start chapter 2 (free account)" twice (A2main/21). The memory they take to bed is "it wanted my email", not the ₹150-crore moment.
- **Reassurance at the email moment:** strong. Orange box: "To open chapter 2 of How Indian IPOs work. Chapter 1 stays on this phone whatever you choose." Lede: "No card." Note: sender named, 20 seconds, 10 minutes, "No other emails, ever, unless you turn reminders on." The one wobble is the sender itself: "a gmail.com address" (SignIn.tsx:109) is honest and reads like a phishing tell to a careful reader.

## 5. What's working

1. **Tap to chapter with no splash.** Card lifts with "Opening…" at 350 ms (A2main/05), chapter at 1,475 ms from the landing, 490 ms from the Shelf (A2x). Cover swing and page turns are transform-only and cheap.
2. **The frames.** Every one of 14 frames carried one idea, legible at arm's length, with the key phrase marked in the second ink. The story bars, "Chapter 1 of 7", and × are exactly enough chrome.
3. **The wall's words.** "Your email and a 6-digit code, no card. Chapter 1 stays on this phone whatever you choose, and your place is kept on any phone or laptop." (Done.tsx:102) is the truest sentence on the path, and the price note under the card ("Chapter 2 costs nothing…") keeps money out of the free card.

## 6. Priority issues

1. **[P0] Every picture is a 2.4–3.9 MB PNG at 1792×1344, shown at 343 px, no srcset.** Measured live: chapter 1 = 19.6 MB over six pictures; the three carousel covers in the first viewport = 11 MB; the Done wall card adds ~3.6 MB. Landing.tsx:53, Chapter.tsx:305, Done.tsx:97, Shelf.tsx:82 are all plain `<img src>`. Why it matters: Jordan is on mobile data; the "already there" Reel promise fails on every screen, and tonight's two-frames-ahead warm-up (Chapter.tsx:139–150) pulls another 7 MB in the background on 4G. Fix: at upload, write a 800 px WebP (about 60–90 KB) and a 400 px thumbnail; serve `srcset` with `sizes="(max-width: 480px) 90vw, 400px"`; keep the PNG for /print only. Command: **optimize**.
2. **[P1] "Not now" lands on a page that asks twice.** After declining, the plan's Up next button (Plan.tsx:54) and the action bar (Plan.tsx:134) both read "Start chapter 2 (free account)"; App.tsx:352 sends the decliner there with no state. Why it matters: 8 finishers, 0 sign-ups; the decline path is the majority path and it ends on a nag. Fix: remember the decline on the device for the night; the plan's main button becomes "Read chapter 1 again ▸", the Up next card says "Chapter 2 opens with a free account. When you're ready." as a quiet line, and the Shelf is offered as a real next step ("Pick another handbook"). Command: **onboard**.
3. **[P1] The wall card hides its own reassurance.** At 390×844 the card's picture plate runs y 690–1085, the h2 is at 1200, and "Chapter 1 stays on this phone whatever you choose" is at ~1450 under a bar fixed at 726 (A2main/15, eval: upnext bottom 815, bar 726). Fix: remove the picture from the wall card (or make it a 56 px thumbnail beside the kicker), so title, hook, and the no-card line fit above the button on first paint. Done.tsx:95–105, index.css:869–876. Command: **layout**.
4. **[P2] Print-language leaks on the Operate screens.** Two pressed elements on sign-in (why-here `--press-sm`, index.css:888, plus the main button); Shelf type at 12.5 px and 9.9 px (index.css:971–972); two finish buttons on the last frame (Chapter.tsx:310, :321); "See pricing" and "I already have a password" are `.quiet` with `padding: 0`, about 20 px tall (Done.tsx:107, SignIn.tsx:118) despite tonight's 44 px pass. Fix: why-here flat with an ink outline; book-title at `--small`, book-goal at `--small` on two lines or dropped; drop the body "Finish chapter 1" and let the tool-row "Finish" be the one; 44 px rows for text links. Command: **polish**.
5. **[P2] Trust copy at the email moment.** "a gmail.com address" (SignIn.tsx:109); "or use a password" offered to people with none (SignIn.tsx:36); "Trending" printed on every carousel card when the Trending pill is active (Landing.tsx:41, A2main/01) with 70 visitors; "Hot" ribbon in coral on a coral book (index.css:973, A2main/23). Fix: send from an igetit.now address (Prateek's call, needs domain mail) or print the exact sender so it can be matched; drop the password fallback when `flow === 'signUp'`; cap "Trending" to the top three; ribbon in ink on coral cloth. Command: **clarify**.

## 9. Three provocative questions

1. PRODUCT.md §4 defines first value as "the first exercise of chapter 1 passed", and the landing now says "No quizzes tonight" (Landing.tsx:177). The wall therefore comes before the product's own definition of value. What is the reader supposed to have proven to themselves when you ask for their email?
2. Why does the wall card carry a picture of a chapter the reader cannot open, when the only sentence that converts is "no card, and chapter 1 stays on this phone"?
3. If "Not now" leads to two more sign-up buttons, is /stats measuring sign-ups, or measuring how many people will read the same request three times before leaving?
