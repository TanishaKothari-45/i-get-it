# Style anchor

Picked by Prateek on 5 Oct 2026 from a style test (three styles × three scenes from Public speaking chapter 1, on Runway's Muse and Gen-4 models). Every chapter picture is drawn from this file, word for word. Change this file first, then redraw the whole set.

Model: Runway `muse_image`, ratio 1792:1344 (4:3), 1 credit a picture. Gen-4 wrote text into pictures despite the brief; Muse did not.

Medium: three-colour risograph print, marigold, indigo and ink on cream paper, visible grain and slight misregistration, bold simple shapes, halftone shading, flat graphic figures with no detailed faces.
Palette: paper cream #faf7f0 (background), soft ink #1b1a17 (lines), marigold #f2a93b (accent), muted indigo #3a4170, deep green #1f7a4d, coral #e0735a.
Light: soft warm daylight from the left, flat print light, no hard shadows.
Materials: uncoated paper, ink grain, halftone dots.
Mood: warm, clear, a little playful.
Composition: 4:3 frame, one subject in the centre and lower two thirds, calm open space at the top, no borders.
Never: any text, letters, numbers, logos or captions; photorealism; 3D render; glossy surfaces; lens flare; neon; gradient-mesh backgrounds; floating particles; stock-photo poses; recognisable real people; anything that must be exact (a chessboard position, a chart, a map, a diagram, a formula). Exact things are drawn by the app, not the image model.

## Added 8 Oct night (art-direction pass)

How a picture sits in the app (the print language, DESIGN.md): every picture is pasted on as a plate, a 3 px paper mat with an offset print shadow, never full-bleed. Covers (the Shelf, the landing carousel, Jump to next) are always a drawing from this anchor; a real photo (Wikimedia, with its credit) may appear inside a chapter but never as a cover, because one press photo next to the drawn books reads as two products (convex/shelf.ts coverOf).
