# Content plan: Instagram and X, 7 to 17 Oct 2026

For I Get It. Written 6 Oct by the agent from PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md, PROGRESS.md and the Build Sprint handbook. Everything here is a plan. Every post is written by Prateek.

## 0. Three rules before anything else

1. **Your words, not the agent's.** The handbook is plain about it: "Never write posts with AI." This plan says what to post, when, in what format and how it looks. It gives you questions to answer (section 8), never captions to paste.
2. **Promise only what's built.** Shaktimaan's rule for the landing page holds on socials too. If the app can't do it tonight, no post says it can (section 3, claims).
3. **One link, tagged by source.** Every link carries `?utm_source=`, so /admin shows which post brought which reader (section 7).

## 1. What this is for

The job: get strangers from a post to chapter 1 passed, tonight, on their phone.

The numbers that count (Virality rows, the heaviest first):
- Signups or meaningful actions, 25x. 101+ is L3.
- Visitors, 10x. Needs read-only analytics or caps at L2 (see section 10, call 2).
- Reactions 2x, impressions 1x.

The anti-spoof maths sets the floor: visitors can't be more than impressions ÷ 10, and signups can't be more than visitors ÷ 2. So 101 signups needs at least 202 visitors, which needs at least 2,020 impressions. Aim for 10x that: 20,000 impressions, 1,000 visitors, 101+ signups by Fri 16 Oct. These are aims, not forecasts. Nothing has been measured yet beyond the 6 Oct reel.

Ten DMs to ten named people beat one post to everyone. Posts make you findable; DMs make readers.

## 2. Who we're talking to

The person from PRODUCT.md section 1, described by situation, not age or title:
- Has saved dozens of "learn this" reels and posts. Follows creators who teach. Nothing resurfaces.
- Has started at least one of these and stopped.
- Would say "I keep meaning to learn X" without being asked.
- Feels a push when people around them start using the thing (agents, most often).
- Worry that stops them: "If I start here I'm skipping levels. I don't have a plan."

First ring: GrowthX coursemates and the cohort. Second ring: the nine from Orbitshift. Then strangers.

Every post should make this person think "that's me" or "I could do that tonight". If it does neither, cut it.

### Look

The socials look like the landing page: a printed risograph poster.

- Colours (DESIGN.md section 3): paper #FAF7F0, ink #1B1A17, marigold #F2A93B, indigo #3442B8, green #1F7A4D, coral #E8604C. Marigold means "this is the way forward": use it for the one call to action or the one highlighted phrase, never for decoration. Brick red (#B3402B) never appears on a post.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque at its heaviest and narrowest for headlines; Newsreader for quotes and longer lines. Both are free on Google Fonts. No other fonts.
- Texture: paper grain, slight misregistration, hard ink outlines, offset print shadows. The headline can carry the off-register indigo shadow from the landing hero.
- Pictures: drawn from design/style-anchor.md, word for word. Three-colour risograph, flat figures, no detailed faces. Never text inside a generated picture; put text on top in Bricolage. Never photoreal, 3D, neon, gradient mesh or a recognisable real person.
- Screens: real screenshots of the live app at 390 px wide. Never a mockup of something that isn't built.
- Money, health and legal topics: any post showing one carries the app's own line, "Study aid, verify before you act."
- Grid rhythm on Instagram: rotate full-bleed colour spreads the way the landing does (marigold, ink, cream, indigo, coral), so the profile grid reads as one poster.

## 4. Four kinds of post

| Kind | What it is | Share | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Learn one thing | A real chapter's frames as a carousel or reel, ending on its quiz | 40% | The post is a free sample. The product is already shaped like Instagram. |
| B. The itch | The saved folder, the half-finished course, "I keep meaning to" | 25% | The reader sees themselves before they see the product. |
| C. Build in public | Real numbers, real bugs, real fixes, decisions | 25% | X's home crowd and the GrowthX cohort. Honest and specific. |
| D. Reader proof | A reader's Done screen, teach-it-back reply, words (with their yes) | 10% now, more later | The only post that proves it works for someone who isn't you. |

## 5. Platform playbook

### Instagram (the trust channel)

- Feed: one post a day, 7 to 17 Oct. Carousels at 1080 × 1350 (4:5), 7 to 10 slides. Reels at 1080 × 1920 (9:16).
- Reel shape: screen recording of a real chapter on your phone, swiping up, with your voice over it. Your own teardown (DESIGN.md 2b, note 8): the first seconds decide it, so open on the hook frame, never on a logo.
- Carousel shape (kind A): slide 1 the hook frame, slides 2 to 6 one idea each, slide 7 the quiz ("Which one? Answer in the comments"), last slide the headline and "Link in bio".
- Stories: three a day. One is always the question sticker: "What do you keep meaning to learn?" Reply to every answer by DM with the link and "Type exactly what you wrote here."
- Comments: reply to every one inside the hour on launch day, same day otherwise.

### X (the builder's channel)

- One post a day from your account, plus 10 replies under bigger accounts in learning, AI and building in public. Replies in your words, adding something, never "check out my app".
- Formats: single posts with one real screenshot; short threads (3 to 5 posts) for build stories; native video for the GTM video.
- Tag @GrowthX_Club on build-in-public posts. Link in the last post of a thread, not the first.

## 8. Questions to answer in your own words

Answer one of these out loud or into a voice note, then cut it to the post. That's the post.

- Kind A: What surprised you in this chapter? What would you tell a friend in one sentence?
- Kind B: What's the oldest thing in your saved folder? Why haven't you opened it?
- Kind C: What broke this week, how did you find out, and what did you change?
- Kind D: What did the reader say, word for word? What did they do next?
- The GTM video (Sat 10): What did you build, who is it for, why would they care, and where's the link? (the handbook's four parts). Show one real night: type a topic, the plan, one quiz passed, the rung lights.

## 10. Calls only you can make

1. **Track.** This plan aims at Virality's rows. Visitors and signups also feed Revenue's signups row, so it isn't wasted either way.
2. **Analytics.** The rubric names PostHog, Plausible, GA4 or Datafast for the visitors row. /stats and /admin live in Convex. If the reviewer won't accept those, visitors cap at L2. Adding any of the four is a new service and needs your yes.
3. **Instagram account.** The handbook says a new account, not your personal one. Was the 6 Oct reel on a new account?
4. **Link.** A .convex.site link in a bio looks unfinished. Convex can serve a custom domain (the convex-domains skill), but you'd buy the domain from a registrar first. Your call.
5. **Share card.** The Done screen is the moment people would screenshot. A proper share card is parked ("anything social"). Worth revisiting after the coursemate test, because it's what the Virality track rewards.
6. **Your face.** Reels with a face on camera earn trust faster than screen-only. Your call.
