# The weekly content system (from Sat 10 Oct)

One fixed shape a week, so posting never depends on having an idea that day. Prateek records every day (8 Oct: "I want to record everyday"); the agent writes the script the night before, then edits, captions and readies the post the same day. Every reel follows the reel formula; every post follows the post format (𝙄 𝙂𝙚𝙩 𝙄𝙩 in bold italic, app-first hashtags). Topics: learning AI and building with it leads (Vibe coding with Claude Code; Prateek builds I Get It this way, so it's his most credible subject). Stocks, bonds and mutual funds and Public speaking are weekly guests (Tue and Thu). One lead subject lets Instagram learn who to show the reels to (docs/growth-playbook.md, rule 7); compare by topic at day 30.

## The recording template (every reel Prateek records)

| Part | Time | What happens | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-3 s | One line that makes the viewer feel they're about to miss something. Said to camera. | The same line as big text, from the first frame |
| Step | 3-20 s | One idea, said simply, with one example | Captions (2 s or more per line) |
| Rehook | about halfway | Cut to the app on the phone screen: the chapter card that says the same thing | The screen recording, beat drop |
| Payoff | last 5 s | Why it's worth saving or sending: "Send this to the friend who…" | |
| CTA | end | One action only: "Chapter 1 is in the bio. Free, no sign-up." | End card with www.igetit.now |

Length 20-40 s. No intro, no "hey guys", no pauses. Speak the hook first, then everything else.


## Retention checklist (check every edit against it)

- **0-1 s:** already mid-sentence; no logo, no "hey guys". The on-screen text names the payoff and the topic word. Something moves. The first frame works as the cover.
- **1-3 s:** a tension or a specific number ("Two commands and one sentence."). This is the window Instagram's skip rate measures.
- **3-10 s:** the first useful piece, fast. Something changes every 2-3 s: a cut, a punch-in, a caption appearing.
- **Middle:** one idea, one concrete example (from the real chapter). Captions burned in. Every pause and filler word cut.
- **End:** the payoff, then one ask: a send ("send this to the friend who…") or "Night N tomorrow". No outro.
- **Loop:** the last line leads back into the first; stop the instant it lands.
- **After posting:** read skip rate and where the retention line drops; fix that exact second in the next reel.

## The daily loop (about 25 minutes of Prateek's time)

| When | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| By 10 PM the night before | Agent | Tomorrow's script in docs/launch/scripts/<date>.md: hook, the one idea, the app screen to record, payoff, CTA, plus the caption |
| Morning, any time before 1 PM | Prateek | Record: 3 takes of the hook alone, then 2 full takes; plus a phone screen recording of the app card the script names (www.igetit.now/?utm_source=internal). AirDrop to the Mac and say "recorded" |
| By 5 PM | Agent | docs/launch/edit/edit.py: pauses cut, hook, rehook cut to the screen recording, captions checked against the script, end card; the finished reel and its caption, checked frame by frame |
| 7 PM IST | Prateek | Post (or tell the agent to post it from Chrome); stories with the link sticker; reply to comments for the first hour |

The one-page recording guide (settings, light, sound, delivery, the 10-minute routine): docs/launch/how-to-record.md.

Setup that stays the same every day: phone vertical at eye level, window light on the face, quiet room, the same spot and top all week so the feed looks like one show. Leave 2 s of silence before and after each take. No intro, no "hey guys".

The safety net: keep 2 finished reels banked (one 60-second lesson, one common mistake). On a day recording can't happen, a banked one goes out at 7 PM and gets replaced the next day. A missed day breaks the streak the algorithm rewards; a banked reel doesn't.

## Hook bank (rotate; don't invent new ones daily)

Countdown:
- "N days left in 2026. How's that 'learn AI this year' going?"
- "N days left. Still nodding along when someone says 'mutual fund'?"
- "N days left. You promised you'd stop freezing in meetings."

60-second lesson:
- "Most people use Claude Code wrong on day one. Here's the fix."
- "A bond is just a loan. You're the bank."
- "Your talk needs one summit, not five."

Common mistake:
- "Stop asking AI to 'make it better'."
- "Stop starting your talk with 'thank you for having me'."
- "Stop nodding along when someone says 'index fund'." (teach what it is, never what to buy)

Building in public:
- "Day N of building an app alone. Today it broke."
- "126 people came. 0 signed up. Here's what I changed."

Proof:
- "This week: N readers, N finished chapter 1, ₹N made. Everything, no filter."
