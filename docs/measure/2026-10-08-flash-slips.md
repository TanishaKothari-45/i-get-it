- How tides work (misleading): "as the Moon's pull moves the bulge of water in" (Bay of Fundy). The 16-metre range comes mainly from the bay's shape amplifying the tide.
- How tides work (misleading): "Earth's daily spin under these bulges makes tides arrive about fifty minutes later". The daily delay comes from the Moon moving along its orbit, not the spin alone.
- How compound interest works (misleading): "A 1,000 dollar deposit earns more money in its second year". Only with compound interest and the first year's interest left in.
